## Description

Stop hunting through the Dock or overlapping windows just to reach an app you use all day.

BoltLauncher gives each Mac app a global keyboard shortcut. Press it from anywhere to open or switch to the app. If it is already frontmost, press the same shortcut to hide it and return to your previous task.

SET UP IN SECONDS

1. Add an app.
2. Record a shortcut you will remember.
3. Use it from anywhere on your Mac.

BUILT FOR FOCUS

• Open, activate, or hide apps with one shortcut
• Choose a different global hotkey for each app
• Keep BoltLauncher ready in the menu bar
• Launch automatically when you sign in
• View simple launch counts stored only on your Mac

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

BoltLauncher works entirely offline. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your selected apps, permissions, shortcuts, and launch counts stay on your Mac.

Requires macOS 13 or later.
