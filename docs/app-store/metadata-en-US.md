## Description

Open, switch, or hide any Mac app—and launch a complete Work Scene—with one global keyboard shortcut.

Assign a hotkey to an app and use it from anywhere. If the app is already frontmost, the same shortcut hides it. Otherwise, BoltLauncher opens or activates it.

WORK SCENES

Group the tools for a project or routine under one shortcut. A Work Scene can open multiple apps plus optional websites, files, and folders. Press the shortcut again to hide the scene's apps without closing its websites or files.

BUILT FOR FOCUS

• Open, activate, or hide apps with one shortcut
• Launch complete work setups from the menu bar or keyboard
• Keep BoltLauncher ready and optionally start it at login
• View simple launch counts stored only on your Mac

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

App shortcuts work offline. BoltLauncher has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Your selected apps, permissions, shortcuts, scenes, and launch counts stay on your Mac. Scene websites open in your default browser.

Requires macOS 13 or later.

## What's New in 1.1.0

Work Scenes are here. Open a group of apps and optional websites, files, or folders with one shortcut. Press it again to hide the scene's apps. This update also improves app activation and file access reliability.
