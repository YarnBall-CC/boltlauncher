## Description

BoltLauncher is a focused menu bar utility for opening the Mac apps you use most.

Choose an application, assign a global hotkey, and use that shortcut from anywhere. If the app is already frontmost, the same shortcut hides it. Otherwise, BoltLauncher opens or activates it.

Features:

- Add applications you choose
- Assign memorable global hotkeys
- Open, activate, or hide apps with one shortcut
- Launch BoltLauncher automatically when you sign in
- Keep simple launch counts locally on your Mac
- Work entirely offline with no account, analytics, or tracking

BoltLauncher uses the macOS App Sandbox and stores its settings only on your Mac.
